The dashboard has two modes. They share one map, one set of correlation rules, and one alert ranking. What differs is where the data comes from, when it is from, and how much of it there is. Nothing in either mode is simulated or relocated: every marker is a real report from the provider named below.

Say this out loud in the demo

The Hormuz circle in Live shows the strait today.

Not 18 August. The Aug 18 picture exists only in Replay. Switching modes changes the date, not just the data.

Nothing is simulated or relocated.

Where a source has no coverage, such as AIS in the Gulf or live radar, the layer is absent rather than faked.

Replay looks busier because it is deeper, not because it is different.

Live holds one aircraft snapshot per minute and 30 minutes of news. Replay holds every position and every window of a full day.

Full provenance, access type, license, and rebuild commands for every source: docs/DATA_SOURCES.md in the fusion-engine repo. Counts verified 2026-09-08.
